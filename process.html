Our Process

We start early, stay close, and build competitive college applications over time.

01

Identify promising sophomores

Meridian works with promising high school sophomores who show ambition, academic potential, work ethic, and readiness to grow. Starting in sophomore year gives students time to build a real record instead of rushing through the application process at the end.

02

Match fellows with mentors

Each Meridian Fellow is connected with mentors who help them think clearly about academics, extracurriculars, essays, testing, leadership, and long-term goals. The relationship is personal, consistent, and built around the student's specific path.

03

Build the application before senior year

We help fellows make thoughtful choices throughout sophomore and junior year so their applications become stronger over time. That includes course planning, activity development, writing support, school research, interview preparation, and a disciplined application timeline.

04

Offer support at no cost

Meridian Fellows receive free tutoring, consulting, and mentoring. We also offer free standardized testing boot camps so students can prepare with structure, accountability, and confidence regardless of their family's ability to pay.
