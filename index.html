M

A nonprofit fellowship for founders, organizers, educators, and builders of public life.

What

The Meridian Fellowship is a year-long program for people building institutions, projects, and coalitions that make civic life more capable, humane, and free.

Why

01

A different path for service

"The place God calls you to is the place where your deep gladness and the world's deep hunger meet."Frederick Buechner

Many people feel called to serve before they have a clean title, a polished institution, or a perfect plan. The fellowship gives them disciplined time to clarify the work and the courage to begin.

02

Freedom to do the work

"Never doubt that a small group of thoughtful, committed citizens can change the world."Margaret Mead

Fellows receive mentorship, practical formation, and project support without being forced into a single model. We help clear the path so builders can focus on the work only they can do.

03

The network is the work

"We cannot live only for ourselves. A thousand fibers connect us with our fellow men."Herman Melville

The hardest part of public leadership is rarely talent. It is finding the right collaborators, advisors, funders, and peers at the right time. Meridian surrounds fellows with people who can help.

As built for

Founders

For people starting nonprofits, civic ventures, mutual aid projects, and mission-driven institutions.

Organizers

For leaders bringing neighbors, congregations, schools, and local partners into durable common work.

Builders

For operators who believe public life improves when people make useful things together.
