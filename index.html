The Program

A year for people ready to serve with clarity.

The Meridian Fellowship is designed for nonprofit founders, organizers, educators, public-interest technologists, and community leaders who want to turn conviction into durable institutions.

  1. 01

    Discern the work

    Fellows begin with a practical inquiry into place, power, obligation, and the communities they are called to serve.

  2. 02

    Build alongside mentors

    Monthly intensives pair strategy, governance, fundraising, communications, and personal leadership practice.

  3. 03

    Launch a civic project

    Each fellow leaves with a tested initiative, a clearer operating model, and a network committed to their next chapter.

What We Cultivate

Four practices for leadership that lasts.

N

Neighborly imagination

Seeing the whole person, the whole block, and the whole system before rushing to solutions.

S

Strategic craft

Turning values into repeatable decisions, resilient programs, and clear measures of progress.

C

Common life

Practicing disagreement, cooperation, and institution-building across lines of difference.

R

Rooted renewal

Building from local strengths so communities can grow without losing their memory or dignity.

Leadership is not a spotlight. It is the patient art of helping people find a shared horizon.
